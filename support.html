The app isn't reading my speed or position

This is almost always a location-permission issue. Check the following on your iPhone:

  • Location permission. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, make sure Location Services is on, then find Pace Zone Monitor in the list and give it permission.
  • Precise Location. In the same screen, make sure Precise Location is enabled. Without it, iOS supplies only an approximate position, which is not accurate enough for speed and zone monitoring.
  • Low Power Mode. iOS limits background activity when Low Power Mode is on, which can affect location updates.
  • Signal. GPS accuracy drops indoors, in tunnels, and in underground car parks. Try again with a clear view of the sky.

How is my data handled?

Everything stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics, and your location is never transmitted anywhere. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your device, removing the app deletes everything it stored. There is no account to close and nothing for us to erase on your behalf.

Reporting a problem

If something looks wrong, a short description of what you expected and what actually happened is usually enough for us to reproduce it. Screenshots help. If the issue happens while driving, please stay safe and send the details afterwards.
