Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version Pace Zone Monitor does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app uses stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Information we collect

None. Pace Zone Monitor does not collect personal information, does not create user accounts, and does not send any data to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers that receive data from the app.

Location data

The app requests access to your device's location in order to work. Location is used solely on your device, in real time, to determine your current speed and your position relative to the zones you are monitoring.

  • Location data is never transmitted off your device.
  • It is not sent to us, and not shared with any third party.
  • It is processed in memory while the app is in use.

You can grant or revoke location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services on your iPhone. If you revoke access, the features that depend on your speed and position will not be able to function.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics frameworks, no crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party software development kits that collect information about you. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data storage and retention

Any settings or data the app keeps are stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage area. Because we never receive this data, we cannot access it, retain it, or delete it on your behalf. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it stored.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will be revised.
