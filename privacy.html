Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version PaceZone does not collect personal data for the developer. Trips and settings stay on your device. Apple Maps and the App Store provide map and purchase services under Apple's own privacy terms. There are no accounts, developer servers, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Information we collect

None for the developer. PaceZone does not create user accounts or send trips, speed measurements, or location history to us. We operate no servers that receive this data from the app.

Location data

The app requests access to your device's location in order to work. Location is processed on your device to determine your current speed and position relative to monitored zones.

  • Your trip and location history are not sent to the developer.
  • Current location is processed on the device for speed and zone detection.
  • The map uses Apple Maps to request map tiles and road routes. Apple may process service requests under its own privacy policy.

You can grant or revoke location access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services on your iPhone. If you revoke access, the features that depend on your speed and position will not be able to function.

Purchases

PaceZone Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. The app receives purchase status from StoreKit so it can unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's purchase and payment data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics frameworks, no crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party software development kits that collect information about you. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data storage and retention

Any settings or data the app keeps are stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage area. Because we never receive this data, we cannot access it, retain it, or delete it on your behalf. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it stored.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will be revised.
